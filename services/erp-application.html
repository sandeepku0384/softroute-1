FAQ

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about this service.

We have certified expertise in Oracle ERP Cloud, Oracle E-Business Suite, SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, Microsoft Dynamics 365, NetSuite and custom-built ERP systems. Our platform-agnostic advisory ensures we recommend the right system for your needs, not the one we're most comfortable with.
Implementation timelines depend on scope and organization size. Small-medium implementations typically take 4-9 months. Enterprise-wide implementations range from 12-24 months. We use phased rollout strategies to deliver value incrementally and reduce risk.
We use a rigorous 5-step data migration methodology: data audit, cleansing and standardization, ETL development, parallel validation and cutover execution. We perform at least two full dress-rehearsal migrations before go-live and always have a tested rollback plan.
We provide a structured hypercare period (typically 30-90 days) with on-call support, daily stand-ups and rapid issue resolution. After hypercare, clients transition to our ongoing support contract which includes help desk, monthly health checks and quarterly optimization reviews.
