How We Work

Our Outsourcing Onboarding Process

From first conversation to full operational handover — a structured process that gets your outsourced team running at full capacity in weeks, not months.

1

Discovery & Scoping

We map your current operations, identify outsourcing candidates, assess risk and design the optimal scope, team structure and transition plan — all before signing anything.

2

Team Assembly & Vetting

We select and vet candidates from our talent pool, run skills assessments, and present you with team profiles. You interview and approve every person who joins your outsourced team.

3

Knowledge Transfer & Training

Structured knowledge transfer — SOP documentation, system access setup, tool training, shadowing periods and certification — ensuring your team can operate independently with confidence.

4

Parallel Run & Validation

A controlled parallel run period where our team works alongside your existing team — building confidence, catching edge cases and proving operational readiness before full handover.

5

Full Operations & Continuous Improvement

Full ownership of the outsourced function — with SLA-driven performance, real-time dashboards, weekly reporting, monthly reviews and continuous process improvement built into the engagement.
